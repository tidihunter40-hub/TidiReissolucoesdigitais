# Imagens demonstrativas geradas para o Studio

Estas cinco imagens foram geradas com a ferramenta de imagem integrada do Codex, convertidas para WebP e salvas localmente. São usadas apenas como conteúdo ilustrativo dos modelos; não representam empresas, pessoas ou produtos reais.

- `band-1.webp`: “A believable diverse five-piece band performing on stage, vocalist in foreground, guitarist, bassist and drummer visible, energetic live show atmosphere. Wide horizontal editorial photograph, faces and instruments fully inside the frame, warm gold stage beams and subtle haze. No text, logos or watermark.”
- `radio-1.webp`: “Contemporary Brazilian radio broadcast studio, professional microphones, mixing console, acoustic panels, headphones and soft indicator lights. Wide horizontal editorial architectural photograph, no people, legible screens, text, logos or watermark.”
- `recording-1.webp`: “Inviting music production room with recording microphone in booth, mixing desk, studio monitors and acoustic treatment. Wide horizontal architectural editorial photograph, warm moody practical lights, believable equipment. No text, logos or watermark.”
- `petshop-1.webp`: “Charming healthy dog and cat in a clean pet shop aisle with visible unbranded pet food bags and bowls. Wide horizontal editorial photograph, natural daylight, animals fully in frame. No text, logos or watermark.”
- `feed-1.webp`: “Neat retail display of generic unbranded bags of feed for dogs, cats and birds, bowls and natural grains, in a professional pet food shop. Wide horizontal realistic commercial photograph, warm natural light. No readable text, logos or watermark.”
